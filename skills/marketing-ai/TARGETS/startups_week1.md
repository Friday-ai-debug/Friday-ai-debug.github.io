# Initial Target Companies - AI Email Assistant

## Strategy Overview

**Target Markets:**
1. Startups (5-50 employees) - Priority 1
2. Agencies (Marketing, Consulting) - Priority 2
3. Growing Companies (50-500 employees) - Priority 3

**Geographic Focus:**
- Philippines (primary - timezone advantage)
- US West Coast (tech hub)
- APAC (growing startup ecosystem)

**Timeline:**
- Week 1: Startups (highest growth potential)
- Week 2: Agencies (understand value, high pain)
- Week 3: Growing companies (established revenue)

---

## Priority 1: Startups (Week 1)

### Target Profile
- **Size:** 5-50 employees
- **Stage:** Seed to Series B
- **Funding:** Recent rounds preferred
- **Growth:** Hiring, expanding teams
- **Pain:** Scaling email processes, hiring faster than can onboard

### Value Props for Startups
- "Scale without hiring"
- "Move faster" - respond to prospects in minutes
- "Never miss a lead" - 24/7 coverage
- "Team productivity" - share snippets, collaborate
- "Fund-raising ready" - quick responses = investor relations

4. Kalibrr

**US Startups (Funding/Growth):**
1. Clerk (recent Series A)

3. Vercel
4. Supabase

### Research Notes

**For each company:**
1. Visit website and about page
2. Check recent funding announcements (TechCrunch, Crunchbase)
3. Identify team size and growth
4. Find LinkedIn profiles of founders/execs
5. Identify email/contact info
6. Research current tech stack
7. Find pain points (recent posts, interviews, complaints)

## Priority 2: Agencies (Week 2)

### Target Profile
- **Type:** Marketing, Consulting, Creative, PR
- **Size:** 10-100 employees
- **Growth:** New client acquisition
- **Pain:** Team productivity, billable hours, client response times

### Value Props for Agencies
- "Serve clients faster" - AI responses = client satisfaction
- "Reduce admin overhead" - focus on billable work
- "Scale team efficiently" - per-seat pricing grows with team
- "Win more proposals" - fast response = competitive advantage

## Priority 3: Growing Companies (Week 3)

### Target Profile
- **Size:** 50-500 employees
- **Stage:** Established, scaling
- **Revenue:** $5M-$50M
- **Growth:** New departments, regional expansion
- **Pain:** Process standardization, team communication at scale

### Value Props for Growing Companies
- "Enterprise-grade productivity" - scales with organization
- "Standardize processes" - consistent email handling
- "Train at scale" - onboarding new employees efficiently
- "Reduce overhead" - measurable cost savings
