Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free trial?

Full access to all features for 30 days. No credit card required. See how much time your team saves before committing.

How do you count seats?

Seats are tied to team members who actively use the AI assistant. Read-only access doesn't count toward your seat limit.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What's the difference between annual and monthly billing?

Annual billing gives you 20-25% in savings compared to monthly. Both options include the same features and support.

Is there a minimum seat commitment?

The Starter plan has a minimum of 1 seat. Team and Business plans have minimums of 4 and 11 seats respectively.
